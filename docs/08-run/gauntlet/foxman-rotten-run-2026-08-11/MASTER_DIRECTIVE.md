## Goal And Definition Of Done

Turn the existing Foxman Phaser game into one polished, replayable,
side-scrolling roguelite vertical slice without damaging the accepted campaign.
Done means a 15-25 minute seeded run with meaningful route and reward choices,
at least four materially different weapon approaches, three active-skill
approaches, eight meaningful mutations or upgrades, five readable enemy roles,
two elite variations, one final boss, death/restart, results, local records,
three independently proven viable builds, original production-coherent
presentation and audio, deterministic regression coverage, real browser play,
blind-critic passes, a local product page, development story, postmortem, and
continuation handoff.

## Frozen Promise

Foxman crashes through a different rotten bureaucratic gauntlet each run,
turning weapons, spiteful skills, mutations, and route choices into a build that
can survive one ugly final reckoning. Movement remains sharp, combat remains
readable, and the comedy comes from the corrupt world and Foxman's terrible
judgment rather than from obscuring play.

## Scope Ceiling

In scope:

- A separate Rotten Run mode built from existing campaign systems.
- A compact seeded route graph with at least three stages and a final boss.
- Four weapon approaches, three active-skill approaches, eight mutations or
  upgrades, five enemy roles, two elites, and only the encounters required to
  prove three viable builds.
- Run-local economy, reward choice, death/restart, results, local records, and
  accessibility/settings required by the slice.
- Original assets, VFX, audio, UI, logo/mode mark, tests, performance evidence,
  product page, development story, postmortem, and handoff.

Out of scope:

- Replacing or deleting the authored campaign.
- Full metaprogression, account systems, multiplayer, backend, monetization,
  native packaging, public production deployment, or an unlimited procedural
  campaign.
- Content expansion after the bounded slice proves its promise.
